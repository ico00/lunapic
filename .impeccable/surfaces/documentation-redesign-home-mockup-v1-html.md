## Direction contract

THESIS: The home screen answers "will a plane cross the Moon, when, and am I standing right" before any panel opens, by drawing the transit as a line on a transit diagram. It refuses the glass-panel space dashboard with twelve unlabelled rainbow icons.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-blue enamel ground, flat opaque panels (no blur, no glow, no gradient), porcelain-white ticks and text, four line inks with fixed jobs: amber = the Moon, cobalt = observer and in-frame, scarlet = disc transit, green = live and ready. Humanist signage sans (Overpass) with tabular numerals; stations are small rings, interchanges are large rings; geometry bends only at 45 and 90 degrees. The selected line burns brightest, the rest dims.

STORY: Photographer in the dark or at a desk sees the next transit headline with a live countdown, a station diagram of the moments (now, enter disc, alignment, exit), a cross-track position check, and the other candidates. Then reaches any of the twelve panels by labelled group.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900. Full-bleed map. Top bar 56px (logo, command search, location and weather chips). Left: tool rail with labelled groups (Now, Shoot, Sky and place, Feeds, Plan), then a 392px Next transit board holding headline, station diagram, position check, frame readiness, primary action, candidate list. Top right: compact Moon and conditions strip with caution state. Bottom centre: time ribbon drawn as a transit line with tonight's candidates as stations. Bottom right: altitude legend and layers. Signature move: time ribbon and board diagram as transit lines.

FORM: Wayfinding cartography midnight transit diagram, assigned (seed:4ac5ed575dc04299), candidate 5 of own list.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
